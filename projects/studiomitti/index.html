Questions people ask

Do you charge for the first visit?

No. The site visit and first concept discussion are free in Delhi NCR.

Can I live in the house during work?

For single rooms, yes. For full homes we recommend moving out for 6 to 8 weeks, and we phase the work if you cannot.

How do payments work?

10% to start, then payments linked to stages such as carpentry done and finishing done. The last 10% is paid after handover.

Can you work with my own architect or furniture?

Yes. We can take over only the execution, or only the design.
