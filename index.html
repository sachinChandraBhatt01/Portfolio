The story so far.

I'm Sachin — a fresher Social Media Manager, Thumbnail Designer, and Video Editor from India. Zero client work yet, and I'm saying that with total honesty.

It started with a simple question: why do some small creators feel bigger than brands with millions of followers? That question sent me deep into platform algorithms, content psychology, thumbnail A/B testing, and video retention analytics.

I've built skills across content strategy, editorial calendars, community management, short-form video editing (Reels, Shorts, TikTok), thumbnail design in Canva and Photoshop, and social analytics via Meta Business Suite, YouTube Studio, and LinkedIn Insights.

What sets me apart is the full-stack creative package. I don't just manage posts — I design the thumbnail that earns the click, edit the video that holds attention, then track the data that tells us what to do next.

I'm actively looking for my first opportunity — freelance, internship, or a small brand ready to grow. Let's talk.
